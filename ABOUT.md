# Orbit 🛰️

### Settle into London fast. Save money. Make the most of it.

Moving to a new city for uni is exciting — and overwhelming. There's a hundred
things to buy, admin to sort, and money leaking everywhere. **Orbit does the
thinking for you.** Tell it one thing — how much energy you've got today — and it
hands you a ready-made plan for your day: where to go, what to get, and the
student discounts to use along the way.

---

## The one-liner

> Orbit is a map-first guide for students new to London that turns "settling in"
> into a simple daily plan — with student discounts built into every stop.

---

## How it works

### 1. Tell us the basics (once)
Pick your **area** (near your uni or halls) and your **budget vibe** —
*Cheap*, *Mid*, or *Premium*. That's it. No sign-up, no account, nothing to
install. Your choices stay on your device.

### 2. Get your day plan 🗺️
Each day, Orbit asks a single, friendly question:

> **"How much do you have in you today?"**
> 😮‍💨 Take it easy &nbsp;·&nbsp; 🙂 A normal day &nbsp;·&nbsp; ⚡ Let's get a lot done

Then it builds you an itinerary that actually fits your energy:

- **Low** → a gentle 2-stop day
- **Medium** → a balanced 3–4 stops
- **High** → a productive 5–6 stops

Every plan starts at your front door, puts the **urgent stuff first** (SIM, bed,
kitchen), always slots in **somewhere to eat**, and ends with something fun to
**explore** — all mapped as a sensible route so you're not criss-crossing the
city.

### 3. Save money without trying 💸
Student discounts show up **right where you'd spend** — on the stop itself.
Heading to IKEA for a duvet? Orbit tells you about the student offer before you
pay. Each stop shows a rough cost, and your plan tallies up an estimated spend
and **how much you saved**.

### 4. Make it yours
Not feeling a stop? **Skip** it. Want a different option? **Swap** it. Tick
things off as you go — Orbit remembers what's done so it won't send you back.

### 5. Or just explore 🧭
Not in the mood for a plan? Open **Explore**, tap a category — *Room & Bedding*,
*Kitchen & Food*, *Events in London* — and browse curated spots on the map,
filtered to your budget, discounts shown inline. Wander at your own pace.

## Why students love it

- **Zero overwhelm.** One question in, a whole day planned out.
- **Money-smart by default.** Discounts surface at the moment you'd pay.
- **Built for London.** Real neighbourhoods, real shops, mapped routes.
- **No sign-up, no faff.** Open it and go. Works great on your phone.
- **Honest.** Every discount shows when we last checked it, and reminds you to
  confirm on the retailer's site.

## The vibe

Clean, calm, map-first. Think a friendly local who's done this before, drawing
you a little map on a napkin — "go here first, grab lunch there, then go see
this." That's Orbit.

---

## Good to know

Orbit is an early MVP. It's **London-only** for now, discounts are curated by
hand (so always double-check the current offer on the retailer's site), and it
focuses on your **first day/week** essentials plus a bit of fun. Accounts,
multi-city, and multi-day planning are on the roadmap.
